# Themed loot candidates

Review list for biome-themed dungeon loot pools. Nothing here is wired in yet.

Source: the jars in the MMSLive01 "MMS 26.3 Test" Prism instance, read from lang files, item models, recipes and loot tables (no code was run or decompiled). Item ids come from lang keys and model files, so check an id in-game before it goes into a pool. Tier is a first guess: common, uncommon, rare, epic, endgame. `endgame` means it could break progression if found early and should only sit in the deepest or boss tables.

Where a mod's own structure loot lists an item, the "why" column says so, since that is the mod's own signal of value.

Mods with no natural biome:

- `wizards` staves, wands, robes, tomes and scrolls
- `paladins` weapons, shields, armor, librams and scrolls
- `relics_rpgs` trinkets and `runes` stones and pouches
- `weaponsexpanded`, `expanded_weaponry`, `basicweapons` weapons
- `fantasy_armor` sets not tied to a biome above
- `mutantmonsters` boss items, `friendsandfoes` totems and music disc
- `uselessreptile` pet and mount items, `direbats` fangs, `takesapillage` items
- `spell_engine` generic scroll and book

`endgame` items (`aerialhell:god_sword`, `god_totem`, `netherian_king_sword`, `arsonist_*` armor, `relics_rpgs` superior relics) should stay out of general pools and only appear in boss or deepest-tier tables.

## 3. Summary of skipped mods

- Libraries and APIs: Fabric API, Cloth Config, owo-lib, GeckoLib, Architectury, Balm, Cardinal Components, Trinkets, Terrablender, Cristel Lib, Lithostitched, Placeholder API, playeranimator, Resourceful Lib, Collective, PuzzlesLib, Ranged Weapon API, Armor Model API, and similar.
- Performance and rendering: Sodium and extras, Lithium, FerriteCore, C2ME, ModernFix, Entity Culling, ImmediatelyFast, Iris (disabled), Distant Horizons (disabled), and similar.
- UI, QoL and utility: JEI, WTHIT, Xaero maps, AppleSkin, Mouse Tweaks, Controlling, Chat mods, Carry On, Better Combat and Combat Roll (combat mechanics, no items), and similar.
- Structure-only mods: Deadly Deadly Dungeon, Towerinator, Towns and Towers, Luki's structure mods (Ancient Cities, Crazy Chambers, Grand Capitals, Strongholds, Woodland Mansions), Command Structures. They add loot tables only, no items (Dungeons and Taverns is the one exception, via keys).
- Farming, food and decor: Farmer's Delight and its addons (Brewin and Chewin, Chef's, Easter's, Rustic, More, Storage, Tweaks Delight), Fish of Thieves, Bountiful Fish, Bubblellaneous, Furnish, Sknirofurniture, Oh The Biomes We've Gone (boats, wood sets, wreaths; only music discs are valuable), Nemo's Blooming Blossom, Variants and Ventures (spawn eggs only).
- Origins mods: Origins Legacy has only the Orb of Origin, which is not loot.
- Enchantment mods: the Expanded Weapon, Axe, Bow, Crossbow, Mace, Trident and Armor Enchanting mods add enchantments, not items. Anvil Never Too Expensive, Enchanted Utilities, Shield Upgrades, Combat Amenities and Axes Are Weapons add nothing lootable.
- Disabled jars and outdated duplicates (`.disabled` suffix), including `fantasy-weapons`.
- Our own `mms-*` jars (arsenal, combat, economy, jobs, metro, weapons, vanity and others), except mms-dungeons, which is covered above.
- Casino, mob battle, minigame and admin mods: Mine Vegas Casino, Mob Battle, and similar.

Hard to judge:

- `dnt` keys: they only make sense next to their own structures.
- `fantasy_armor`: every set is cosmetic with unknown stats, so tiers are guesses.
- `relics_rpgs`: ids are lang keys that look like perk names, so confirm against the registry.
- `biomeswevegone`: nothing valuable beyond two music discs, so it has no section.
- `weaponsexpanded`, `expanded_weaponry`, `basicweapons`: heavy overlap; consider using only one of them in loot to avoid duplicate-looking drops.
- Item ids for AC `radon_bottle`, `diving_*`, `hazmat_*` and similar set pieces are inferred from sibling lang keys.
